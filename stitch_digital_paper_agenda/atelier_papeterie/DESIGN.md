## Brand & Style

This design system translates the sensory prestige, contemplative pace, and exacting craftsmanship of bespoke bookbinding and high-grade archival stationery (Hobonichi Techo, Smythson, Leuchtturm1917) into a digital artifact. The visual language firmly rejects ubiquitous SaaS conventions—no floating translucent plastic cards, no bright pill-shaped chips, and no neon interactive states. Instead, the interface operates as a physical stationery spread resting on an artisan's dark walnut writing desk.

The experience is targeted at connoisseurs, writers, horologists, and archival planners who value deliberateness, tactile physical feedback, and typographically disciplined layouts. The atmosphere is warm, studious, intellectual, and quiet. Every visual boundary is defined by paper grain, bookcloth binding gutters, faint lithographic or letterpress rules, foil stamping, and liquid fountain pen ink absorption.

## Colors

The palette reproduces real-world raw stationery materials: vegetable-tanned leather, heavy Japanese Tomoe River cream paper, gallic iron ink, and beaten bookbinder brass.

- **Primary (`#1E2229`) — Midnight Iron Gall Ink:** Deep archival blue-black ink with high carbon saturation. Used for primary text entries, key dates, primary borders, and prominent lithographic rulings.
- **Secondary (`#5C5248`) — Soft Sepia Graphite:** Warm lead-pencil and diluted walnut-husk ink tone. Used for subtle metadata, inactive ledger rules, micro timestamps, secondary notes, and minor headers.
- **Tertiary (`#8C2D19`) — Carmine Stamp Red:** Traditional vermilion vermeil and wax seal hue. Used exclusively for priority markings, margin vertical rules, alert stamps, and callouts.
- **Accent Green (`#2D5A46`) — Hunter Green Stamp:** Forest bookcloth and rubber stamp ink for completed milestones, approved ledger items, and verified entries.
- **Gilded Accent (`#C5A059`) — Bookbinder’s Brass & Gold Leaf:** Brushed brass page markers, foil-stamped headers, ribbon bookbinder weaves, and gilded page edge trims.
- **Base Canvas Desk (`#2B2621`):** Rich oiled walnut and patinated hide desktop backdrop against which the ledger rests.
- **Paper Surface (`#FBF8F2` & `#F5EFE6`):** Warm unbleached cotton pulp base page (`#FBF8F2`) transitioning to parchment edge aging (`#F5EFE6`).
- **Faint Rulings (`#E2D9CC`):** Watermark-density horizontal feint lines and 3.7mm register dot grids.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy simulates hot-metal letterpress, cold copperplate engraving, and mechanical typewriter stamps.

- **Headlines (`EB Garamond`):** Traditional, proportioned, graceful old-style serif. Generous vertical breathing room matches classical folio book composition. Numerals are old-style where supported.
- **Editorial Sub-headers & Small Caps (`EB Garamond` with high tracking):** Set in tracked small capitals (`caption-caps`, `title-editorial`) to evoke hand-set French stationery labels and vintage chapter rubrics.
- **Body & Diary Text (`Newsreader`):** Exceptional clarity at small body sizes with editorial warmth. The `body-script` variant utilizes italics (`font-style: italic`) to simulate natural, unhurried hand-written diary cursive in wet fountain pen ink.
- **Ledger Stamps & Time-Markers (`Courier Prime`):** Monospaced typewriter precision evokes date stamps, mechanical ledger records, and time intervals (e.g., `07:30`, `REF: 4920`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model mimics an open, Smyth-sewn binding spread resting upon a desk surface:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Fixed dual-page folio display (verso on left, recto on right) centered against the dark walnut canvas (`#2B2621`). The central seam represents the book gutter with sewn signatures, flanked by an outer edge showing die-cut index tabs along the right margin. Maximum page spread container width: 1360px.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Compact dual-page with compressed inner gutters, or single-page turn format with persistent right-aligned index tabs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-page view (recto or verso selectable via edge ribbons). Canvas padding reduces to 1rem to prioritize maximum readable paper surface.
- **Horizontal Ledger Rhythm:** Page body content adheres strictly to a baseline grid multiple of 24px (1.5rem) or 28px (1.75rem), ensuring hand-written text aligns perfectly with printed ruling lines (`#E2D9CC`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved strictly through physical paper physics, bookbinding structures, and tactile stationery finishes:

1. **The Desk Surface Backdrop:** Solid rich walnut tone (`#2B2621`). Non-reflective and ground-level.
2. **Book Edge Deckle & Drop Shadows:** The open ledger casts a multi-layered, diffused ambient shadow onto the wood: `0 4px 12px rgba(10, 8, 6, 0.45)`, layered over `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(10, 8, 6, 0.65)`.
3. **The Spine Gutter:** At the division between left and right pages, a subtle two-sided radial gradient creates the shadow of an opened bound valley: left page right edge features a linear gradient from `transparent` to `rgba(43, 38, 33, 0.12)`, right page left edge reflects a corresponding shadow, accompanied by a razor-thin 1px central crease in `#D4C9BC`.
4. **Die-Cut Thumb Tabs:** Outer page index tabs have an under-cast edge shadow (`2px 2px 5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.18)`), creating physical elevation above the current open page layer.
5. **Debossing & Letterpress Inset:** Section dividing rules, date stamps, and brass insignia use a crisp 1px inset highlight (`text-shadow: 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` or `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 2px rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.25)`), creating the illusion of deep steel-die embossing into soft, heavy paper stock.

## Shapes

The design system enforces architectural, guillotine-trimmed paper edges with sharp corners (`0px` roundedness / level `0`).

- **Pages and Columns:** Perfectly crisp rectangular cuts representing trimmed archival sheets.
- **Deckled Exceptions:** Outer deckle-edge styling utilizes organic sub-pixel edge masking rather than arbitrary modern border-radii.
- **Die-Cut Index Tabs:** Outer right edges possess a subtle angular notch or specialized 4px tab chamfer strictly simulating physical die-cut cardboard or laminated index paper. Modern rounded "pills" or continuous radius bubbles are strictly prohibited.
- **Checkboxes:** Rigid, micro-fine squares (12px by 12px) bordered with 0.75pt ink outlines, occasionally rendered with slight hand-drawn asymmetry.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Affordances
- **Foil-Stamped Button:** Rich brass/gilded outline (`1px solid #C5A059`) with faint cream background (`#F5EFE6`). Text is set in `caption-caps` in `#1E2229`. On hover, subtle brass foil sheen shift (`#B89045`) and 0.5px ink deboss.
- **Red Ink Seal Button (Action/Confirm):** Simulates an ink seal or wax stamp. Inset border or circular/octagonal stamp motif in Carmine Red (`#8C2D19`). Pressed states shift color density like wet ink.
- **Ghost Literary Actions:** Plain serif text with an authentic printer's ornament (such as `—` or `§`) that underlines with an uneven ink stroke upon hover.

### Die-Cut Index Tabs
- Vertically stacked tabs extending from the right edge of the notebook spread: `DAILY`, `WEEKLY`, `MONTHLY`, `PROJECTS`, `MEMORANDA`.
- Base material: Aged parchment (`#EFE8DC`) with letterpress ink typography (`label-stamp`). Active tab mirrors the open paper color (`#FBF8F2`) and visually merges flush into the page boundary without an edge line.

### Ledger Grids & Ruled Lines
- **Horizontal Ledger:** Continuous parallel 0.5pt rulings in `#E2D9CC` spaced at 24px increments.
- **Margin Rule:** Single vertical line running top-to-bottom in soft vintage Carmine Red (`#8C2D19`, opacity 0.4), set 48px from the left page margin.
- **Dot Grid:** 1.5px dots placed precisely at 5mm (or 20px) intervals in `#DED5C7` for sketching and unconstrained note-taking.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- No boxed input containers. Text fields are represented as blank horizontal ruling lines.
- Focused state introduces a subtle dark-ink blot or thin caret resembling the nib of a fountain pen.
- Placeholder text is rendered in Sepia Graphite (`#5C5248`) in italicized `Newsreader`.

### Checkboxes & Progress
- **Habit/Task Squares:** 12px square outlines with 0.5pt border in Midnight Ink (`#1E2229`).
- **Completed State:** Filled with an authentic hand-inked cross (`✕`) or fountain-pen wash in Hunter Green (`#2D5A46`) or Midnight Ink (`#1E2229`), maintaining slight organic variation.

### Bookmarks & Ribbons
- Woven silk page marker ribbons simulated as vertical floating strips (`#C5A059` brass gold or `#8C2D19` crimson) draping down the gutter crease and hanging below the bottom edge of the paper pages with an angled cut tail.
